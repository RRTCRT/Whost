What we collect

Information you give us

  • When you use our contact form: your name, email address, phone number, and whatever you write in the message.
  • When you sign up for the referral program: your first and last name, email address, phone number, and any message.
  • When you place a custom order: your name and contact details, delivery address, and the measurements needed to make the garment fit you — chest, waist, sleeve length, calf, and similar.
  • When you bring us a repair: your name and contact details, plus a description of the item.
  • When you call or email us directly: whatever you choose to tell us.

About measurements. We ask for body measurements only because a made-to-measure garment cannot be cut without them. We use them to make and fit your order, we keep them so repeat orders and later alterations fit the same way, and we do not share them with anyone. If you would rather we deleted them once your order is finished, say so and we will.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our host records basic technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser type, the pages you looked at, and the time of the visit. This sits in server logs and is used to keep the site running and secure.

Cookies

  • This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies of its own.
  • WordPress may set a cookie if you leave a comment or log in as a member of staff.
  • If online ordering is switched on, our shop software sets cookies to remember what is in your basket. These are necessary for the shop to work.

Video, maps and other embedded content

Some pages include content served by other companies. When you load a page containing it, that company can see your IP address and may set its own cookies, exactly as if you had visited their site directly.

  • YouTube — our videos are hosted on YouTube. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and our video players do not load anything from YouTube until you click play.
  • Google Maps — the maps on our Locations and Contact pages are served by Google.
  • Etsy — some product pages link to our Etsy shop. Etsy has its own privacy policy.
  • Social media — we link to our Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and YouTube pages. Following a link takes you to their platform and their rules.

We do not control these companies' privacy practices. Their policies apply once you interact with them.

Who else sees your information

Only the people who have to:

  • Our website host stores the site and its data on their servers.
  • Our email provider carries messages sent through the contact form and our replies.
  • Our phone service — our (605) number is a Google Voice line, so call and message records pass through Google.
  • Shipping carriers receive the name and address needed to deliver your order.
  • Payment processors, if you pay online. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, if we are legally required to hand something over.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never have.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information, where we are not required to keep it;
  • stop sending you anything you did not ask for.

Email roadkillleather@gmail.com or call the shop. We will not charge you, and we will not treat you any differently for asking.

California residents. If you live in California, the CCPA gives you the rights above, plus the right to know whether we sell or share personal information. We do not, and we do not sell the personal information of anyone under 16.

Visitors from the UK or EU. If you contact us from outside the United States, your information will be handled in the US. You have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to our use of it, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we keep the software behind it up to date. No website can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable care with what you give us, and we do not keep more of it than we need.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be flagged on the site.
