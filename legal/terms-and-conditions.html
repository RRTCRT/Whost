Our products are handmade

Everything we sell is cut and sewn by hand in our own shop, from natural deer, elk, bison and cow hide. That has consequences worth stating plainly:

  • No two pieces are identical. Grain, colour depth and texture vary between hides and between dye lots.
  • Natural marks are not defects. Scars, scratches, bites, brands and healed wounds are part of the hide's history. On our rustic range they are the point. Holes are decoratively patched, which adds character rather than hiding it.
  • Screens lie about colour. The colour you see on your phone or monitor will not exactly match the leather in your hands. If an exact shade matters, call us and we will describe it, send a photo, or post a sample.
  • Sizes are a guide. Leather relaxes and moulds to its wearer. Where fit is critical, we recommend a custom order.

Repairs and alterations

When you leave an item with us, it stays your property and we treat it as such. But repairing worn leather carries risks we cannot remove:

  • Old leather can fail. Aged, dried, sun-damaged or previously repaired leather may tear, crack or split during work, sometimes in a place nobody could have predicted. We will assess the item and warn you if we think it is fragile, but you accept that risk when you leave it with us.
  • We do not guarantee pre-existing damage. A repair fixes the fault we agreed to fix. It does not warrant the rest of the garment.
  • Patches: we sew patches, we do not make them, and we have no embroidery capability. We can sew onto pockets without closing them for an additional charge. Placement is agreed before we start and cannot be undone without leaving needle marks.
  • Colour and hardware may not match exactly. Replacement snaps, zippers and thread are matched as closely as we can, but an exact match to aged leather is rarely possible.
